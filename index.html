Welcome to D. N. Patel College of Engineering

Campus Photo

D. N. Patel College of Engineering, Shahada, was established in 1983 by Poojya Sane Guruji Vidya Prasarak Mandal to promote technical education among rural and tribal students. The college is currently affiliated with Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU), Lonere, and is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi. With a strong focus on quality education, industry-oriented training, and overall personality development, D. N. Patel College of Engineering offers various undergraduate and diploma programs in engineering disciplines such as Computer Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and more. The institute is committed to creating skilled professionals and responsible citizens to meet the challenges of the modern technological world.

About D.N.Patel COE

P. S. G. V. P. Mandal’s D. N. Patel College of Engineering was established in 1983 by the Honorable Sahakar Maharshi Shri. Annasaheb P. K. Patil with the mission to mould the young generation to the new technology of high order that can meet the challenges in the fast-developing technological world by shaping the discipline, competence, and character of technocrats. The college has a progressive management team led by Shri. Bapusaheb Dipakbhai Patil, President, PSGVPM, Shahada. This college was the first A-grade college in North Maharastra University, Jalgaon, and is presently affiliated with Dr.B.A.T.U. University, Lonere. The college has a glorious past with recognition from different educational agencies. AICTE has offered NBA accridiation and NAAC, Bangalore has conferred a ‘B’ grade in the 1st cycle.P


The college provides instructions for various undergraduate courses in the streams of Civil, Computer, E & TC, Electrical, Instrumentation, and Mechanical engineering. The teaching faculty consists of 54 learned faculty members and experts in their own subject, discharging their duties with a sense of dedication and devotion.

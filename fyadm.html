First Year Admission Process

The process of admission through CAP and the eligibility criteria is specified, documented and published by DTE for given AY. The admission process through CAP changes from time to time. As in the admission process from 2012 to 2024, merit list was based on the score obtained in CET. In the admission process for 2024-25, allotment is based on the composite score (combination of marks in the qualifying exam and JEEMains or CET) obtained by the candidate. In general, admission process consists of following activities :

1. Registration, document verifications, and confirmation at ARC.
2. Display of merit list.
3. Option form filling for institute of interest.
4. Allotment of seats through CAP rounds.
5. Reporting to institute.

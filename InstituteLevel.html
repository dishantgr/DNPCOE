Admission through Institute Level

At institute level, the admissions from IQ are either in Management Quota and/or against CAP vacant seats. The admission process consists of following activities:

The admission process is based on the policies and guidelines provided by Director of Technical Education (DTE), Maharashtra State, Mumbai.

1. Invitation of applications for available seats.
2. Generation and display of merit list.
3. Notification for allotment of seats.
4. Confirmation of admission by candidate.
